Changing the target resets it. The 2 mm setting resolves shock fronts best but runs slower, especially on phones. The plate length is adjustable; tilted plates are shortened to fit the picture. Bolted jaws grip the top and bottom 32 mm, and the bolts are tightened before every shot. Rigid mounting freezes those strips in place instead. The model is plane strain, so energies and bolt loads are per 10 mm of depth.

What the model does
  • Each solid is a Lagrangian particle continuum (state-based correspondence) carrying a full stress tensor, with hourglass control.
  • Pressure comes from a Mie–Grüneisen equation of state built on each material's measured linear Us–up shock Hugoniot.
  • Artificial bulk viscosity spreads shocks over a few particles and turns their irreversible work into heat, so shocked material ends up hotter than it started.
  • Metals and polymers follow Johnson–Cook flow stress with strain hardening, strain-rate hardening and thermal softening. Elastic precursors and plastic waves separate on their own.
  • Ductile failure accumulates plastic strain against a stress-triaxiality-dependent limit; tension beyond the spall strength fails material outright.
  • Brittle solids crack perpendicular to the largest principal tension, and comminute under pressure-dependent shear. Cracked faces still carry compression through contact.
  • Contacts between bodies use the same Hugoniot, so the interface pressure matches the impedance-match value.
  • Bolted mounting: steel jaws 24 mm thick grip each end. The rear jaw is fixed to the frame; the front bar is free to move and is pulled tight by M20 grade 8.8 bolts (about 0.2 bolt per 10 mm of depth) through 22 mm clearance holes in the plate. Steel-on-steel friction (μ 0.35) holds the plate until it has slipped the 1 mm clearance; then the bolt shanks bear on the hole walls, and shear off above 47 kN per 10 mm of depth. In tension the bolts stretch elastically, yield at 29 kN (losing preload) and snap after 6 % stretch. Before each shot the assembly is tightened and damped to rest, so the plate starts in a bolted-up equilibrium.
